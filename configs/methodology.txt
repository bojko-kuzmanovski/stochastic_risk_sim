Methodology: Probabilistic Risk Communication (20-day cycle)

Structure:
- Duration: 20 days per use case
- Output: 4 posts (1 every 5 days) + 1 final article
- Goal: Explain, analyze, stress, and propose interventions on a system under uncertainty

Posts:

Post 1 — Phenomenon
Question: What happens?
- Define system
- Identify key variables (independent)
- Describe observed problem / pain

Post 2 — Causality
Question: Why does it happen?
- Identify explanatory factors
- Describe dependencies and distributions
- Outline causal structure

Post 3 — Sensitivity
Question: Where does it break?
- Analyze system sensitivity
- Identify critical variables and thresholds
- Highlight non-linear effects or failure modes

Post 4 — Intervention
Question: What do you do?
- Propose control measures or regulators
- Explain expected impact on system behavior
- Address robustness under uncertainty

Final Article (shared in Post 4):
- Integrates full analysis
- Includes deeper modeling insights
- References sources, methods, and assumptions
- Positions framework as reusable across domains

Principle:
From observation → explanation → instability → decision
